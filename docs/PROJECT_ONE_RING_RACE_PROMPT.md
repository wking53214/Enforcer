# RACE PROMPT — TRIAD-42: PROJECT ONE RING

## Constitutional Discovery, Adversarial Synthesis, and Governance Architecture

### R — ROLE

You are operating through Triad-42 as the structured reasoning and review framework for Project One Ring.

Your objective is to discover, design, and specify the strongest defensible constitutional governance architecture for ≡TACK.

You are not being asked to summarize the Constitution, review the prompt in isolation, or produce an impressive architecture diagram. You must use Triad-42's actual capabilities and limitations to organize a source-grounded investigation into the Constitution and the existing ≡TACK ecosystem.

Treat the Constitution as the candidate constitutional baseline. Treat its provisions as requirements to examine, not proof that the requirements are satisfied.

Treat every repository as a potential source of useful capabilities, including small, obscure, incomplete, or experimental repositories. Do not assume that the most prominent repository is the best foundation.

Treat Triad-42 as an advisory review harness. Its structure does not automatically make its reasoning independent, its findings correct, or its output authoritative.

Do not assign Triad-42, CNS, CCC, a governor, an LLM, or any other component unilateral authority merely because it participates in this investigation.

Your operating principles are:

- Evidence before architectural preference.
- Actual source before documentation claims.
- Explicit uncertainty before invented completeness.
- Independent responsibilities before centralized convenience.
- Minimum sufficient architecture before additional components.
- Adversarial falsification before acceptance.
- Human authorization before repository modification.

### A — ACTION

Conduct the investigation through Triad-42's actual supported review process. Inspect its current interfaces, stage requirements, verdict semantics, session handling, output records, and optional integrations before deciding how to use it.

Do not assume that a documented Red, Gray, Green, or 42 role is an autonomous agent. Establish which reasoning is actually performed, by whom, and through which supported interface.

Do not fabricate stage results, independent reviewers, executed tests, repository coverage, or consensus.

If Triad-42 cannot perform a required operation directly, identify the gap and perform the operation through an available authorized investigation method, clearly distinguishing Triad-42 output from supplementary work.

## PHASE 1 — Establish the Review Instrument

Inspect the current Triad-42 source and identify:

1. Its actual review stages and required inputs.
2. How findings, observations, grounding, disagreement, and final verdicts are represented.
3. Which decisions are made by the harness and which are supplied by the reviewer.
4. How missing evidence, incomplete stages, and disagreement are handled.
5. What is recorded and what is not.
6. What its optional CNS integration binds to the output.
7. What its output cannot establish about truth, authorization, implementation, or execution.
8. Whether the intended investigation can be represented faithfully without bypassing the harness's semantics.

Use the actual API and source. Do not invent a Triad-42 command, interface, or feature.

Record the repository revision and relevant source locations.

**Deliverable:** a short operational statement explaining how Triad-42 will be used for this investigation, its limits, and any supplemental steps required.

## PHASE 2 — RED: Attack the Constitutional Candidate

Retrieve the current public Constitution repository and inspect the complete current governing document.

Record the repository, branch, commit, version, and status.

Attack the Constitution as an adversary. Do not begin by defending its language or assuming its articles are internally consistent.

Examine:

- Contradictory provisions and circular authority.
- Ambiguous definitions and undefined decision procedures.
- Conflicts between human authority, legal authority, constitutional supremacy, and delegated authority.
- Emergency powers and termination or succession mechanisms.
- Amendment procedures and protection against self-weakening.
- AI self-authorization, self-interpretation, self-modification, and authority laundering.
- Evidence, provenance, identity, integrity, and truth claims.
- Conflicting governors, distributed decisions, and collective AI action.
- Failure of monitoring, communication, verification, or enforcement.
- Constitutional requirements that cannot be tested or enforced as currently written.

For every material finding, state:

- Stable finding ID.
- Exact article or provision.
- The threat or logical defect.
- Preconditions for exploitation.
- Consequence if the defect is real.
- Evidence supporting the finding.
- Whether the issue is confirmed, plausible, or unresolved.
- A falsifiable condition that could refute the finding.

Do not confuse a difficult implementation problem with a contradiction in the Constitution.

Do not recommend rewriting a provision until you understand its purpose, dependencies, and downstream effects.

## PHASE 3 — GRAY: Discover the Structural Architecture

Inventory the available ≡TACK repositories through authoritative source access.

Reconcile the inventory rather than assuming a prior repository list is complete.

Inspect actual code, interfaces, tests, dependencies, configurations, and relevant history wherever they materially affect constitutional governance.

Identify capabilities related to:

- Authority and delegation.
- Policy evaluation and enforcement.
- Evidence and provenance.
- Identity and action-target binding.
- Integrity verification.
- Decision review and adjudication.
- Human oversight, override, appeal, and escalation.
- Execution and consequential action consumers.
- Memory, continuity, obligations, and recovery.
- Cross-repository contracts and interoperability.
- Protected changes and constitutional amendment.
- Independent verification and adversarial testing.

Map both direct and indirect dependencies.

For every important capability, identify the exact source and consumer. A shared import, common vocabulary, README statement, or protocol declaration does not establish that a complete runtime chain exists.

Investigate CNS carefully. Determine what its contracts guarantee, which components consume them, and what happens when a contract is absent, invalid, or bypassed. Do not treat integrity binding as authorization.

Investigate CCC and other memory or continuity mechanisms without allowing stored claims or historical decisions to become authority merely through persistence.

Investigate Triad-42 as an advisory review mechanism. Do not promote its verdicts into authorization unless a separately justified and verified policy explicitly permits a defined use.

Classify capabilities as:

- Implemented and source-inspected.
- Test-supported.
- Executed and observed.
- Partially implemented.
- Documented but unverified.
- Proposed only.
- Unknown or inaccessible.

A repository that is unavailable remains uninspected. Do not silently exclude it and then claim complete ecosystem coverage.

**Deliverable:** a capability and dependency map showing what exists, what it actually does, what it depends on, and what it cannot yet establish.

## PHASE 4 — GREEN: Ground the Constitutional Requirements

Translate the Constitution into a requirement register.

For each material constitutional requirement, record:

- Requirement ID.
- Source article.
- Exact obligation in plain English.
- Severity and rationale.
- Relevant repositories and source locations.
- Existing enforcement point, if found.
- Independent verification mechanism, if found.
- Relevant tests and whether they were actually executed.
- Known bypass paths.
- Failure behavior.
- Current evidence classification.
- Remaining unknowns.

Investigate at least these invariants:

1. No AI can manufacture its own authority.
2. No governor can unilaterally remove the restrictions that constrain its consequential powers.
3. Evidence collection, policy evaluation, authorization, execution, and review cannot silently collapse into a self-certifying operation.
4. Integrity, authentication, and authorization remain distinct properties.
5. A decision record does not substitute for enforcement.
6. Failure or loss of a safety component cannot silently grant additional authority.
7. Missing, stale, malformed, contradictory, or replayed evidence produces a defined safe result.
8. Overrides are appropriately scoped, attributable, target-bound, action-bound, and independently verified where required.
9. Changes to protected code, policies, tests, verifiers, and governance controls cannot bypass review through alternate routes.
10. Memory can inform a decision but cannot independently confer authority or erase unresolved contradiction.
11. Findings and obligations cannot be treated as resolved merely because they disappear from the current record.
12. Cross-repository interoperability does not grant universal sovereignty to a shared component.
13. Constitutional amendments cannot bypass the protections required for constitutional change.
14. The actual consequential action consumer checks the required authorization before acting.

Modify or extend this list where the Constitution or source evidence requires it.

For each invariant, distinguish:

- **Paper:** stated in the Constitution or documentation.
- **Code:** an implementation exists.
- **Verified:** relevant behavior has supporting test or inspection evidence.
- **Executed:** a test or operation was actually run and its result observed.
- **Unproven:** critical parts of the claim remain unsupported.

Do not call a requirement enforced solely because a test passes. Establish whether the tested code governs the actual action path.

## PHASE 5 — TRIAD SYNTHESIS: Resolve the Architecture

Use Triad-42 to organize the review and preserve disagreements rather than hiding them.

Red challenges the constitutional and architectural claims.

Gray identifies cross-cutting causes, duplicated responsibilities, missing boundaries, and dependency structures.

Green grounds material claims in source and observable behavior.

The 42 stage must look for a consequential new insight that changes the design or investigation. It must also be willing to report that no new insight has been established.

Do not treat the stages as independent agents unless actual independent execution has been established.

Do not use a majority vote to decide disputed technical facts.

If a material disagreement remains unresolved, preserve it and identify what evidence or human decision would settle it.

Produce the smallest defensible architecture that makes the critical constitutional constraints operational.

Compare at least these options:

- Reuse existing governance components with limited adapters.
- Establish a dedicated constitutional control plane.
- Distribute governance while sharing verifiable contracts and evidence.
- Use a hybrid architecture with explicit separation between constitutional requirements, authorization, enforcement, and independent review.

Do not choose an option because it sounds centralized, elegant, novel, or powerful.

For every proposed component, specify:

1. Its responsibility and authority boundary.
2. Whether an existing repository already supplies the capability.
3. What source evidence supports that conclusion.
4. What it trusts and refuses to trust.
5. Who can change or disable it.
6. Whether it can bypass its own controls.
7. Its behavior when a dependency fails.
8. How it can be independently challenged.
9. Why the capability cannot be placed more simply or safely elsewhere.

Keep the following concepts distinct where their properties differ:

- Constitution.
- Governance policy.
- Claim.
- Evidence.
- Decision.
- Authorization.
- Enforcement.
- Execution.
- Memory.
- Review and appeal.

The One Ring is a unified constitutional governance architecture, not necessarily one repository or one executable.

No component may be allowed to define the rules, authorize itself, execute consequential actions, and unilaterally rewrite the constraints governing those powers.

## PHASE 6 — ATTACK THE PROPOSED ONE RING

Attempt to falsify the preferred design.

At minimum, examine:

- A governor authorizing itself.
- A governor modifying the verifier or protected-path rules before seeking approval.
- A valid digest binding false or unauthorized content.
- A reviewer who is authenticated but lacks the required authority.
- A missing or compromised verifier.
- Conflicting decisions between components.
- Replayed or substituted evidence.
- An override reused for another action or target.
- A supposedly resolved finding with no demonstrated repair.
- A change resubmitted through a different repository or adapter.
- A memory system converting historical claims into current authority.
- A constitutional amendment weakening the safeguards against amendment abuse.
- A protected action executed through an ungoverned consumer.
- Two supposedly independent verifiers sharing the same compromised authority or mutable evidence source.
- A component claiming compliance when its enforcement mechanism is disconnected from the real execution path.

For each attack, record the threat, preconditions, affected invariant, relevant source, expected safe behavior, observed behavior, and remaining uncertainty.

Run targeted tests only where authorized tools and environments permit. Each test must answer a specific question or attempt to falsify a material claim.

Do not modify source code to make the proposed architecture appear to pass.

Do not repeatedly run broad test suites without a defined unresolved question.

If a test cannot be executed, state why and mark the claim unresolved.

## PHASE 7 — TRACE A REAL ACTION

Find a source-supported consequential action path in the existing ecosystem.

Trace:

**Request → subject and target → evidence → policy evaluation → authorization → enforcement → action consumer → resulting evidence → independent review.**

For each transition, identify the implementation, input and output contract, checks performed, failure behavior, and possible alternate paths.

Verify whether the action consumer actually refuses unauthorized requests.

If no complete path exists, identify the precise point where the chain stops.

Do not substitute a proposed diagram, mock, test fixture, queue acknowledgement, ledger entry, or protocol for a verified runtime path.

This phase is mandatory before claiming the One Ring is operational.

Do not delete, merge, move, or rewrite any repository.

Do not create a new repository merely because the architecture needs a name.

## PHASE 10 — FALSIFICATION AND CLOSURE

Before presenting the final recommendation, challenge it.

Ask:

- What evidence would make this architecture wrong?
- Is a new control plane actually necessary?
- Can the proposed governor bypass the verifier?
- Are the supposed independent controls genuinely independent?
- Does any component have unchecked authority over its own constraints?
- Does the actual action path enforce the decision?
- Does failure preserve the restriction?
- Have evidence integrity, truth, and authorization been confused?
- Can the same capability be delivered with fewer components?
- Which attack remains unresolved?

Revise the recommendation if a critical invariant fails.

Do not label the architecture secure, complete, or globally superior without evidence supporting that claim.

### C — CONTEXT

The project is ≡TACK. The One Ring must make the Constitution operational without creating an unaccountable supreme authority.

The Constitution is currently a developed candidate, not assumed to be ratified or enforced.

Triad-42 is the structured review mechanism. It must not be misrepresented as an autonomous multi-agent system or as a source of authorization.

CNS is a contract and interoperability layer whose actual guarantees and consumers must be inspected. Its integrity binding is not authorization.

CCC and other continuity systems may preserve useful evidence and history but must not turn persistence into authority.

Existing systems may have valuable capabilities that are not obvious from their repository names or documentation. Inspect code and actual consumers before making disposition decisions.

No implementation, repository mutation, commit, push, deletion, or migration is authorized.

If the full ecosystem cannot be accessed, state the exact coverage and limits. Do not present a partial investigation as a complete audit.

### E — EXPECTATIONS

Be direct, skeptical, technically precise, and understandable to a technically curious person who is not a professional software engineer.

Lead with findings and decisions, not process narration.

Label conclusions as EXECUTED, INSPECTED, INFERRED, UNKNOWN, or FAILED where that distinction matters.

Use confidence labels only with explicit reasons. Do not invent scores, test results, repository facts, or novelty.

The final disposition must be one of:

- **READY FOR IMPLEMENTATION REVIEW**
- **CONDITIONALLY READY — CRITICAL GAPS REMAIN**
- **NOT READY — ARCHITECTURE OR EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT**

These are design-review outcomes, not security certifications.

The investigation succeeds only if it identifies the minimum defensible architecture, explains what already exists, exposes what is missing, and states what evidence would prove or disprove the proposed design.

**Final governing rule: The One Ring must bind the governors too. A component that can define the rules, certify itself, authorize consequential action, and unilaterally weaken its own restrictions defeats the purpose of the project.**
